Make the allowance last the whole week

Finny keeps a weekly or monthly student allowance in one place: you log what you spend, it shows you where the money went, finds cheaper spots nearby, and answers budgeting questions. Every number comes from you, typed in by hand.

Read this first. Finny is a budgeting and planning aid for students. It is not a bank, a payment processor, a lender, or a certified financial advisor. It never links to a live bank account, moves real money, or offers loans.

Getting started

Four steps, about five minutes. You can change any of it later.

  1. Open the app and read the consent screen

    Work through the welcome screen and the data-privacy notice, then pick which optional permissions and features you agree to. Nothing optional turns on by itself.

  2. Set your allowance and categories

    Enter the allowance or budget you have for the period, then choose categories — food, transport, study materials, personal and leisure — and the threshold where each one should warn you.

  3. Log your first expense

    Tap + Add expense, type the amount, pick a category, and save. A coffee counts; small entries are what make the dashboard honest.

  4. Look around the dashboard

    Open the dashboard for your spending breakdown, ask the assistant a budgeting question, or browse budget-friendly spots near you.

Features

What each part of the app does, and how to use it.

Expenses and allowance

  • Set your allowance. Open Budget / Allowance, enter the starting amount for the period, and save.
  • Log an expense. Tap + Add expense, type the amount, choose a category, add a note or a date if you want, and save.
  • Set alerts. Give a category or the whole allowance a limit, and Finny warns you as you get close and again when you cross it.

Budget-friendly places nearby

  • Find somewhere. Open Location finder and allow location access when it asks.
  • Filter by what is left. Set a maximum you want to spend, and the list narrows to places that fit it.
  • Check before you go. Distance, price-range notes and basic details are on each spot, so an evening out does not eat the rest of the week.

The AI budget assistant

  • Ask about your own numbers. How much is left in food this week, how to pace what remains, where to cut back on things you do not need.
  • Read the alerts and summaries. They catch a fast week early, while slowing down still works.
  • Keep your own judgment. Answers are budgeting guidance, not guaranteed financial, legal or investment advice. Check any figure against your logged expenses.

Dashboard and charts

  • See where it went. The dashboard compares categories against each other for the current period.
  • Watch the balance. Remaining allowance against total logged spending tells you the pace for the days left.

Privacy, consent and your data

  • Review or withdraw consent. Settings → Privacy & Consent, any time, for everything you agreed to.
  • Delete your data. Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete data, then confirm the request.

Questions students ask

Does Finny connect to my bank account, CliQ or e-wallet?

No. You type in your allowance and your expenses yourself. Nothing is linked to a live account and no real money moves through the app.

Is this certified financial advice?

No. Finny organises a student budget, tracks spending and offers general budgeting tips. It is not a licensed financial advisor, a bank or a lender.

Can I change my budget or a category limit halfway through the week?

Yes. Edit your allowance or limits in budget or category settings whenever you like. Your remaining balance and your alerts recalculate from the new numbers.

What happens if I withdraw consent or delete my data?

Both live in privacy settings, and both are yours to trigger. Withdrawing a permission switches off whatever depends on it — location suggestions, for instance, or assistant prompts — and deleting your data removes what the app has stored for you.

When something breaks

Six things that go wrong, and what to check first.

An expense did not save, or the amount is wrong

Check that the amount field holds a number and nothing else — a stray letter or symbol blocks the save — then save again. For a wrong amount already in, open the entry from your expense list and edit or delete it there.

Spending alerts never arrive

Make sure that category or the overall allowance actually has a limit set, and that notifications are on both inside Finny and in your phone's own notification settings.

The location finder shows nothing, or the wrong area

Turn on GPS and confirm Finny has location permission. If the list is still empty, widen the budget filter or move and refresh the map.

The assistant gives a vague or off-target answer

Ask something narrower — name the category and what you have left. Then check any arithmetic it gives you against the expenses on your dashboard.

The dashboard looks out of date or half empty

Confirm your recent expenses were saved to the active budget period and to a category, then refresh or reopen the dashboard.

You cannot find the privacy, consent or deletion screens

They sit under the main menu or the Settings tab, listed as privacy, consent, or account and data controls. If a screen will not respond, close the app, reopen it, and try once more.

Getting help

If your problem is not above, or you want to tell the team a step here is wrong, reach the Finny project team through your course or project support channel. Send these three things and you will get a useful answer faster.

  • Which screen or feature you were on: expenses, location finder, assistant, dashboard or privacy settings.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps — with anything personal cropped out.
